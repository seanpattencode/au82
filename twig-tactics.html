3 BATTLES · 6 UNIT TYPES · DIRECT CONTROL

The art of twig tactics.

Destroy the red statue while keeping yours standing.

01

Start with gold. Miners work automatically, carrying gold back to your statue. Recruit more to grow your income. Gold deposits do not run out.

02

Build a balanced army. Blades are cheap; rangers shoot from behind shields. Sentinels hold the line, mystics blast groups, and titans crush statues. Upgrade at the armory.

03

Choose the moment. Defend holds your line. Garrison recalls and heals everyone and activates statue arrows. Attack sends the army forward; miners keep working.

04

Lead from the front. Tap a soldier, then Take Control. Use the on-screen arrows or A/D to move and Strike or Space to attack. A controlled miner mines with Strike and deposits gold near your statue.
